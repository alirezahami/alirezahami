About Me

I am a final-year Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) student at Isfahan University of Medical Sciences with a strong interest in pursuing a Ph.D. in Antibody Engineering, Cellular Immunology, and Oncology. My current research focuses on the expression and purification of recombinant proteins, specifically the Fc fragment of trastuzumab. I am highly motivated to contribute to therapeutic protein research and biologics development.

Thesis Project Details

Project Title: Expression, purification and in vitro biological evaluation of a split-intein antibody scFv-Fc against HER2.

As part of a broader research initiative aimed at producing trastuzumab using an intein-based protein-expression strategy, my thesis focuses on the synthesis and purification of the Fc fragment of split trastuzumab using a C-intein-based approach.

My specific responsibilities and methodologies in this project include:

  • Performing bacterial cloning, plasmid preparation, and PCR as part of the molecular cloning workflow.
  • Carrying out recombinant protein expression and downstream purification using nickel-affinity chromatography and membrane-based purification.
  • Preparing and evaluating SDS-PAGE gels to assess protein expression and purification efficiency.
  • Planned responsibility for the in vivo functional evaluation of the produced protein in nude mice, scheduled for 2026.
