About Me

I am a final-year Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) student at Isfahan University of Medical Sciences with a strong interest in pursuing a Ph.D. in Antibody Engineering, Cellular Immunology, and Oncology. My current research focuses on the expression and purification of recombinant proteins, specifically the Fc fragment of trastuzumab. I am highly motivated to contribute to therapeutic protein research and biologics development.

Research Interests

I am deeply interested in the intersection of molecular biology and therapeutic development. My primary research interests for future Ph.D. studies include:

  • Antibody Engineering and novel antibody formats (e.g., scFv, nanobodies).
  • Cellular Immunology and immuno-oncology.
  • Recombinant protein expression and purification optimization.
  • Biologics development and therapeutic protein research.
COLLABORATIVE RESEARCH PROJECT

Development of a Split-Intein-Based Anti-HER2 Antibody

A broader project developing an engineered scFv-Fc antibody through recombinant protein production and split-intein-mediated assembly.

01

Fc Fragment

Recombinant expression and purification of the IgG1 Fc component fused to the C-terminal split intein (IntC).

IntC–Linker–Fc
02

Anti-HER2 scFv

Recombinant production and purification of the HER2-specific single-chain variable fragment fused to the N-terminal split intein (IntN).

scFv–Linker–IntN
03

Antibody Assembly

Split-intein-mediated protein trans-splicing to covalently connect the Fc and scFv components, followed by in vitro evaluation of HER2 recognition.

scFv–Fc Antibody
