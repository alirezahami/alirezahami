COLLABORATIVE RESEARCH PROJECT

Development of a Split-Intein-Based Anti-HER2 Antibody

A broader project developing an engineered scFv-Fc antibody through recombinant protein production and split-intein-mediated assembly.

01

Fc Fragment

Recombinant expression and purification of the IgG1 Fc component fused to the C-terminal split intein (IntC).

IntC–Linker–Fc
02

Anti-HER2 scFv

Recombinant production and purification of the HER2-specific single-chain variable fragment fused to the N-terminal split intein (IntN).

scFv–Linker–IntN
03

Antibody Assembly

Split-intein-mediated protein trans-splicing to covalently connect the Fc and scFv components, followed by in vitro evaluation of HER2 recognition.

scFv–Fc Antibody
